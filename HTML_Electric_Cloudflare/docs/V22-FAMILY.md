# V22 family

The V22 family uses the same shared visual contract as the other machines.

- Pro 5: 35MTC20AE, version 2.04, 298 original pages and 162 circuit pages. Dedicated electrical system, I/O, search and source viewer.
- Pro 6: periodic maintenance 16F23A-1606EN (124 pages) and LS adjustment notes (15 pages). These are document viewers only. No Pro 6 electrical drawing bundle was found in the supplied sources; Pro 5 wiring is never reused for Pro 6.
- Eight circuit pages share a sheet number with another page in the original: 323, 351, 352, 441. Internal IDs include PDF page numbers; original sheet labels are preserved. Grey references may link to multiple original variants. These links do not resolve actual machine options.
- Original PDF bytes are split into hosting-sized parts. The viewer verifies SHA-256 before offering the original PDF.
- The machine photo is from the official Makino V22 product page and is labelled as a model reference. It does not establish the machine configuration.
- The machine-specific component list and customer identity document were not included.

Validation: all 437 page images, three reconstructed PDF hashes, source routes, graph endpoints and all 162 circuit models passed local checks. Pro 5 / Pro 6 selection keeps electrical and maintenance scope separate.
